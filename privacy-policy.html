07

Cookies & tracking.

7.1 What Cookies Are

A cookie is a small text file that a website places on your device when you visit. Cookies allow the site to recognize your device on subsequent visits, remember your preferences, and keep you logged in to features that require it. We also use technologies that work similarly to cookies — including web beacons, pixels, local storage, and software development kits — and the term "cookies" in this Section refers to all of these.

7.2 Categories of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly necessary cookies — required for the site to function. These cannot be turned off.
  • Functional cookies — remember preferences such as preferred language, dark mode, or which articles you have already read.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how the site is used in aggregate, which content is most read, and where visitors come from. We use Google Analytics and similar services for this purpose.
  • Advertising cookies — used by us and by our advertising partners to deliver and measure advertising, both on this site and on other sites you visit.
  • Email tracking pixels — tiny image files embedded in our newsletters that allow us to measure whether the email was opened and which links were clicked.
  • Anti-spam cookies — we use Google reCAPTCHA on our subscription form to prevent automated submissions; reCAPTCHA sets its own cookies subject to Google's privacy policy.

7.3 How to Control Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block cookies, delete cookies that have already been set, or alert you when a cookie is being placed. Disabling cookies may prevent some parts of the site from functioning correctly. For advertising cookies specifically, you can opt out of personalized advertising from many networks at the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out tool (optout.networkadvertising.org), the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out tool (optout.aboutads.info), and similar industry programs. Mobile devices typically include a separate setting to limit ad tracking.

7.4 Global Privacy Control

We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal as an opt-out from "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for residents of states whose laws recognize GPC, including California. If your browser sends a GPC signal when you access the site, we will treat that signal as an opt-out request for the device or browser sending it.

Email communications.

When you subscribe to our newsletter, you will receive editorial content and promotional content from advertising partners. Every email we send, whether editorial or promotional, contains a working unsubscribe link. Selecting the unsubscribe link will remove your email from active distribution within the period required by the CAN-SPAM Act (15 U.S.C. § 7701 et seq.), which is no later than ten business days after the request is received.

We retain unsubscribed email addresses on a suppression list, indefinitely or for as long as the suppression file is needed to ensure that we do not contact unsubscribed users again. You can also unsubscribe by emailing support@goldenretirememo.com.

Active newsletter subscribers' email addresses are kept while subscribed; once unsubscribed, the address is moved to the suppression list described in Section 8. Aggregate analytics information that has been de-identified or aggregated is retained for longer periods because it is no longer linked to an identified person.

No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure. If we become aware of a security incident affecting your personal information, we will notify affected individuals and regulators in accordance with applicable law.

Your privacy rights.

11.1 Rights Available to All Visitors

  • You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link at the bottom of any email or by contacting us.
  • You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.
  • You can ask us to stop processing your information for direct marketing purposes.
  • You can withdraw any consent you have given, with effect for the future.

11.2 Additional Rights for California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) gives you the rights described above and, in addition, the following:

  • Right to know what categories of personal information we have collected about you in the past 12 months, the sources, the business purposes for collection, and the categories of recipients.
  • Right to know the specific pieces of personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to delete personal information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to correct inaccurate information.
  • Right to opt out of "sale" of personal information and of "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to limit our use of "sensitive personal information" (we do not currently use sensitive personal information for purposes that would trigger this right).
  • Right not to be discriminated against for exercising any of the above rights.

To exercise these rights, please email support@goldenretirememo.com with the words "California Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will verify your identity using information that matches what we already hold, and we will respond within the time required by law. You may also designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf, in which case we will require written documentation of the authorization.

11.3 Additional Rights for Residents of Other US States

Residents of Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, and Virginia have rights similar to those described in Section 11.2 under their state privacy laws. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@goldenretirememo.com and identify the state in which you reside; we will apply the rights and timelines available under the law of that state.

11.4 Residents of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation, the UK GDPR, and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection respectively grant you specific rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection to processing, data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Golden Retire Memo is operated from the United States and our editorial focus is on US readers; we do not target our newsletter to subscribers in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland. If you are located in one of these regions and wish to exercise these rights, please contact us at support@goldenretirememo.com.

11.5 Residents of Canada

If you are a Canadian resident, you have the rights granted by the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and by your provincial privacy laws. Canadian Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) also applies to commercial electronic messages we send to Canadian recipients; we send such messages only to subscribers who have voluntarily opted in.
