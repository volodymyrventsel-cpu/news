About Golden Retire Memo

About Us.

Golden Retire Memo is a digital publication covering cryptocurrency, decentralized finance, digital-asset markets, foreign exchange, and the macroeconomic stories that drive them. We exist to give readers a clear, useful read on what is moving in crypto, in DeFi, and in the markets surrounding both — without burying the lead in jargon and without pretending to be more certain than the facts allow.

02

What we cover.

Our editorial coverage is organized around four main areas, each of which has its own section on the site:

I

Trading

Coverage of cryptocurrency markets, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, and major altcoins; spot and derivatives markets; technical analysis and price-action commentary; ETF flows; and the on-chain and macro signals that move the market.

II

Forex

Coverage of foreign-exchange markets and the cross-asset stories that intersect with crypto, including currency pairs that trade against the dollar, central-bank intervention, and the carry trade.

III

Stocks

Coverage of public companies with meaningful crypto and digital-asset exposure — mining companies, treasury holders, exchange operators — as well as the broader equity context for crypto-adjacent investing.

IV

Economy

Coverage of the macroeconomic stories that frame the rest of our coverage: inflation, central-bank policy, interest rates, the dollar, and the geopolitical events that move risk assets.

03

Who we write for.

Golden Retire Memo is written for general US adult readers who want a working knowledge of crypto, digital assets, and the markets surrounding them, without going to specialist trade press. Our typical reader holds investments — through a brokerage, a self-custodied crypto wallet, or some combination — but is not a professional trader. They follow the news enough to want context, not just headlines, and they are short on time.

Most of our readership is in the United States; we are accessible internationally but our editorial frame is US-domestic.

04

How we work.

Our editorial team selects the day's stories with two questions in mind: does it actually matter to a US retail investor with crypto exposure, and can we add something — context, plain language, the trade-off underneath the headline — that they would not get from a wire copy. We aim for clarity over cleverness, and we are explicit when we are reporting versus when we are characterizing. Where our coverage includes price predictions or scenario analysis, we treat them as opinion at the time of publication, not as forecasts to act on.

Some of the content in our financial categories is sourced from established crypto-news partners and republished here under licensing arrangements with attribution to the original publisher. Where this applies, we disclose the original source on the article. Editorial commentary and original reporting is produced by the in-house editorial team listed below.
